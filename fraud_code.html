The Strategic Context: As Standard Bank's digital banking footprint grows, so does the sophistication of financial fraud. Traditional rule-based systems generate excessive false positives, frustrating customers and overwhelming our fraud analysts. Sentinel AI addresses this by transforming raw machine learning outputs into actionable business intelligence. Today, out of 170 attempted frauds, Sentinel AI successfully intercepted 154, protecting R3.85 million in customer funds, while ensuring 99.96% of genuine customers experienced zero friction.
🚀 The Strategic Vision
Sentinel AI is not merely a fraud classifier; it is an intelligent fraud operations platform. It empowers Standard Bank to detect, explain, prioritize, and respond to fraud in real time. By balancing rigorous security with a seamless customer experience, we are not just preventing losses—we are actively building trust in digital banking. This platform ensures our fraud analysts are equipped to make faster, more consistent decisions, turning a traditional cost center into a strategic competitive advantage.

It evaluates every transaction, assigns a dynamic probability score, and routes it through a Business Rules Engine. This ensures that human fraud analysts are empowered to make the final call on complex cases, perfectly balancing security with customer experience.

🛡️ Graduated Response to False Positives
When a legitimate transaction is flagged, the bank does not block it permanently. Instead, a graduated response is triggered to preserve customer trust while ensuring security:
1
Transaction is temporarily held (not blocked)
2
Customer receives OTP via SMS or push notification
3
Customer enters OTP or uses biometric verification
4
Transaction proceeds automatically
5
Model learns from the confirmation to reduce future false positives
Result: The 56 false positives today caused zero financial loss and zero permanent blocks. Customers experienced a brief, acceptable 30-second verification step.
